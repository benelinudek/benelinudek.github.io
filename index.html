01 / ABOUT

MY STORY.

BENELI NUDEK

Official photo coming soon

Ben Nudekor Eli, professionally known as Beneli Nudek, is a musician, singer, songwriter, rapper, creative and entrepreneur.

Born on 31 July 2008 in Tegbi, Volta Region, Ghana, Beneli Nudek grew up in Tegbi and developed a strong connection with music, creativity and self-expression.

His creative world moves through Afropop, Afrobeat, street-pop, drill and trap.

Music gives him a way to express emotions, experiences, thoughts and different sides of life that he wants the world to understand.
