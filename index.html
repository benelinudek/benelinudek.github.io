01 / ABOUT

MY STORY.

Ben Nudekor Eli, professionally known as Beneli Nudek, is a musician, singer, songwriter, rapper and entrepreneur.

Born on 31 July 2008 in Tegbi, Volta Region, Ghana, Beneli Nudek grew up in Tegbi and developed a strong connection with music, creativity and self-expression.

His musical world moves through Afropop, Afrobeat, street-pop, drill and trap, combining emotion, personal experiences and different sides of his creative identity.
